About Miraat

A Smarter, More Human Path to Self-Understanding

Our Guiding Question

We started Miraat with a simple but powerful question: Can a digital tool for mental wellness feel less like a test and more like a trusted conversation?

Traditional online assessments often feel impersonal and overwhelming. They ask you to fill out long, static forms, putting the burden on you to figure out where to even begin. We believe there's a better way—a path that is more intelligent, more respectful of your time, and fundamentally more human.

Miraat is our answer. It's a confidential wellness co-pilot, re-imagined from the ground up to partner with you on your journey of self-discovery.

The Miraat Difference: An Intelligent Dialogue

Your experience here doesn't begin with a 50-question checklist. It starts with a simple "How are you feeling?" From that moment on, you are in a dialogue.

👂

We Listen First

After you share your initial thoughts, our AI assistant's first job is to listen and understand the impact on your life.

🤝

We Collaborate

Based on your input, the system identifies potential themes and engages you in a short, collaborative dialogue.

🎯

We Refine, Together

The AI asks just 3-5 targeted questions to reach a clear, high-confidence understanding of your situation.

This process is over 75% more efficient than traditional methods. It transforms a daunting task into a manageable, interactive experience that feels like a guided conversation, not an interrogation.

Engineered for Your Privacy & Safety

Trust is the foundation of any meaningful conversation about mental well-being. We have engineered Miraat with a privacy-by-design philosophy, ensuring your data is protected at every layer.

1

Automated Anonymity

From the moment you start typing, our system automatically finds and redacts Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like names and locations. Your personal story remains private.

2

Unbreakable Security

Your entire session history—from the conversation to the final report—is protected with AES-256 application-level encryption before being stored. This means your data is unreadable gibberish to anyone, even in the unlikely event of a database breach.

3

Proactive Safety

An automated Safety Gate runs silently in the background, designed to detect signs of acute crisis and provide immediate access to emergency resources, bypassing the standard assessment flow entirely.

Our Commitment

Miraat is a demonstration of how modern AI, when architected with care, responsibility, and a human-centered focus, can create powerful new tools for self-awareness. It is not a replacement for a doctor or therapist, but a safe, intelligent first step on your path toward a healthier, more resilient mind.

Your privacy is paramount. Your well-being is our purpose.

Welcome to a smarter conversation.
